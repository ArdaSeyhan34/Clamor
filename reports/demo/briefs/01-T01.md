## The mobile app takes forever to load

*Bug / reliability · theme T01*

### Problem
Customers report: “The mobile app takes forever to load.”

### Who is affected
- **190** mentions from **162** accounts in the last 60 days (534 all time; 17.9% of all feedback in that window)
- Plans: free 54%, pro 32%, business 12%
- Channels: app store 39%, support ticket 20%, nps survey 18%
- Accounts raising it represent **$526,788 ARR**; revenue-weighted demand is $9,290 MRR

### Evidence
- “The mobile app takes forever to load.”
- “The Android app is so slow it is unusable on odler phones.”
- “The app freezes every time I switch calendars on my Samsung phone.”
- “The app freezes every time I swtich calendars on my iPhone.”
- “The app hangs for ten seconds before shownig my schedule.”

### Why now
Its share of feedback is stable: the rate over the last 28 days is 0.96x the baseline (95% CI 0.74-1.22, FDR q 1.000).

Priority score **60.0/100**, rank #1 (#1 if we only counted mentions).

- Release **v3.4** (2026-05-14, Android performance pass): *No detectable change*, mention rate x1.08 afterwards

### Options to explore
1. Reproduce with the accounts behind these quotes and add monitoring on the failing path
1. Ship a targeted fix behind a flag and watch this theme's mention rate afterwards
1. Proactively tell affected accounts what happened and when it will be fixed

### How we will know it worked
- This theme's mention rate drops significantly in Clamor's release radar within 4 weeks of shipping
- Sentiment of remaining mentions improves; no new theme spikes after the release
